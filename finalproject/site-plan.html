Site Plan: National Parks Explorer

WDD 231 Individual Project

Site Name

Name: National Parks Explorer

Reasoning: This name directly communicates the purpose of the site. It is clear, recognizable, and encourages users to explore the content. It represents a hub for discovering and learning about various national parks.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this site is to provide an interactive platform for users to discover, explore, and learn about different national parks. It will list park details, provide dynamic information, and allow users to save their favorite parks to a local list for future reference.

Scenarios

  • Scenario 1: "What are the operating hours and location of Yellowstone National Park?"
  • Scenario 2: "How can I keep track of the national parks I want to visit next summer?"

Color Scheme

The color scheme uses earthy and natural tones to reflect the outdoor and nature theme of the website. These colors are used to style this very site plan.

Primary
#2c5e1a
Secondary
#8c5a2b
Background
#f4f6f4
Text
#333333
Accent
#ff9f1c

Usage:

  • Primary Color (Forest Green): Used for headers, navigation backgrounds, and primary buttons.
  • Secondary Color (Earth Brown): Used for footers, borders, and secondary text elements.
  • Background Color (Light Nature Green/Gray): Used as the main body background to keep it light but thematic.
  • Text Color (Dark Charcoal): Used for all main paragraph text to ensure high contrast and readability.
  • Accent Color (Sunset Orange): Used sparingly for call-to-action buttons (like "Save to Favorites") and hover states to draw attention.

Typography

The site uses two fonts from Google Fonts to maintain a clean, modern, yet welcoming look.

  • Heading Font: 'Outfit', sans-serif. Used for all main headings (h1, h2, h3) and navigation links. It provides a bold, contemporary feel.
  • Body Font: 'Roboto', sans-serif. Used for all paragraph text and lists. It is highly legible and clean.
